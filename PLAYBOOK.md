## 5. Objection Handling Battlecards

### Objection 1: "We already do all our estimating in-house."
> **Response:** *"That makes complete sense—most successful contractors do. We don't replace your in-house estimator; we act as their overflow release valve. When 4 or 5 bids land in the same week and your team is at 100% capacity, instead of passing on jobs or rushing numbers at 2 AM, you send us the plans and we deliver the raw takeoff within 24–48 hours so your estimator only has to review and apply pricing. Would it help to keep us on standby for your next bid surge?"*

### Objection 2: "How do I know your takeoffs will be accurate?"
> **Response:** *"That's why we never ask anyone to take our word for it. We provide 100% transparent, color-coded Bluebeam/PlanSwift drawing markups where every single measurement, count, and deduction is visually verified right on the plan sheets. Plus, on our first job, we do it completely free so you can compare our numbers side-by-side against your own past bids before spending a single dollar."*

### Objection 3: "Do you provide pricing or just material takeoffs?"
> **Response:** *"We provide both depending on your preference. We can provide raw Material Takeoffs (quantities, linear feet, square footage, item counts) ready for your supplier quotes, or full Cost Estimates with labor hours, equipment, and localized material rates using current RSMeans and regional zip-code cost databases."*

### Objection 4: "What is your turnaround time on a commercial set of plans?"
> **Response:** *"For single-trade subcontractors (drywall, painting, roofing, flooring, MEP), our turnaround is typically 24 to 48 hours. For full multi-division commercial general contracting projects, it is 3 to 5 business days, with rush 24-hour turnaround available upon request."*
